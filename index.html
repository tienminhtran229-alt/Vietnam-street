Vietnamese Street Food

Vietnamese street food is famous for its fresh ingredients, simple cooking methods, and strong flavors.

Three Popular Vietnamese Foods

  • Pho
  • Banh Mi
  • Bun Cha

1. Pho

Pho is one of the most well-known Vietnamese dishes. It is made with rice noodles, broth, herbs, and usually beef or chicken.

2. Banh Mi

Banh Mi is a Vietnamese sandwich made with a crispy baguette. It can contain meat, vegetables, herbs, and different sauces.

3. Bun Cha

Bun Cha is a popular dish from Hanoi. It usually includes grilled pork, rice noodles, fresh herbs, and a sweet and sour dipping sauce.
